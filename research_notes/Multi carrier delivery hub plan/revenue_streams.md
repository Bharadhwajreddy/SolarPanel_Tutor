# Revenue Streams and Margins for a Small Multi-Carrier Logistics Operator in Hyderabad (2025-2026)

Labels used: **[Official]** = company's own page or rate card; **[News]** = established news outlet; **[Marketing/Blog]** = franchise-lead sites, SEO blogs, competitor comparison pages (numbers often unsourced); **[Anecdotal]** = case study, job board, individual reports. Research was limited to about 20 web searches and fetches. Many programmes do not publish partner payouts, and those are listed as gaps.

Baseline from earlier research: last-mile hub payouts are about ₹14-25/parcel (unofficial) and rider cost about ₹12-17/parcel.

---

## Q1. Courier aggregator reselling to local sellers, D2C brands and SMEs (Shiprocket, NimbusPost, iThink, Shipway, Delhivery One, ShipGlobal)

### Takeaway
Aggregator wholesale rates for a 500 g domestic parcel start at about ₹19-27 (best zone, highest plan) and average ₹36-45 per shipment on Shiprocket's official plan page. Walk-in counter prices for 500 g are about ₹60-120 (blog estimates). That leaves an inferred gross margin of roughly ₹20-50 per walk-in shipment, and much less (₹5-15) for online D2C sellers, who can sign up to the aggregators directly. None of the aggregators publish reseller or white-label margin terms.

### Cited Findings
- **Shiprocket official plans (2025-26):** Lite is FREE with an average shipment cost of ₹45 (up to 5 orders/month). Business is ₹199/month at ₹41 average (5-50 orders/month). Advanced is ₹499/month at ₹39 (50-200 orders/month). Pro is ₹799/month at ₹36 (3,000-10,000 orders/month). The page shows a ₹1,000 first-recharge promo that gives ₹1,600 in the wallet (code FLAT600). **[Official]** — [Shiprocket Pricing](https://shiprocket.in/pricing/)
- Shiprocket's pricing page lists a "Become a Partner" programme and a "Refer & Earn" scheme ("Refer sellers to Shiprocket and unlock exciting rewards"). No commission figures are published. **[Official]** — [Shiprocket Pricing](https://shiprocket.in/pricing/)
- Third-party listings put Shiprocket's starting rate at "as low as ₹27/500 g" — [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/shiprocket). Another gives ₹23/500 g — [XeroWP](https://xerowp.com/plugins/shiprocket). **[Marketing/Blog]**
- Older third-party listings show plan prices of ₹1,000, ₹2,000 and ₹3,000/month, which conflicts with the current official ₹199, ₹499 and ₹799. The older data is outdated. — [Shiprocket KB (undated)](https://www.shiprocket.in/knowledgebase/which-plan-should-i-purchase/); [PricingSaaS 2026](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/shiprocket)
- **NimbusPost** starting prices are ₹19-26 per 500 g across plan tiers, with the lowest rate on the highest-volume plan. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [FitGap NimbusPost](https://us.fitgap.com/products/nimbuspost)
- In a 2022 report, NimbusPost's founder said the company earns from SaaS fees and "cost arbitrage", works on a 20-25% gross margin, and gives volume discounts to larger customers. This is the only published aggregator margin figure found. **[News, 2022]** — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/?p=176043)
- NimbusPost signed an MoU with India Post in 2024 to integrate with its APIs. The deal gives access to 156,434 post offices and serves 80,000+ sellers. **[News]** — [ITLN](https://www.itln.in/logistics/nimbuspost-partners-with-india-post-to-boost-order-fulfilment-1348761)
- Shipway says it has 11+ couriers and 30+ channel and warehouse partners. Its entry rates are about ₹19-26/500 g, similar to NimbusPost's. **[Marketing/Blog — competitor comparison page]** — [Shipway vs NimbusPost](https://shipway.com/shipway-vs-nimbuspost); Shipway's first-recharge promo is "Add ₹1000 & Get ₹1700" — [Shipway](https://shipway.com/nimbuspost-alternatives)
- **Walk-in counter prices for 500 g (DTDC-type counters):** Blog estimates are about ₹60-90 intra-state and ₹80-120 inter-state. Another source gives ₹150-250 for zonal/national and ₹50-120 for same-city delivery. Documents up to 500 g are about ₹60-100. A fuel surcharge of about 15-25% of base is added, plus remote-area and re-attempt fees. **[Marketing/Blog; sources conflict]** — [iThink Logistics DTDC charges](https://www.ithinklogistics.com/blog/dtdc-courier-charges/amp/); [ClickPost DTDC charges](https://www.clickpost.ai/blog/dtdc-courier-charges); [Shiprocket DTDC charges](https://shiprocket.in/blog/dtdc-courier-charges)
- Borzo's January 2025 blog puts general Indian shipping charges at ₹30-90 per 500 g. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [Borzo blog](https://borzodelivery.com/in/blog/courier-charges-in-india-a-guide-to-shipping-costs)

### Inferences
- **Walk-in retail reselling:** A seller who walks in pays about ₹60-100 for a 500 g intra-state parcel. The operator books it on an aggregator at about ₹25-45 (Zone A/B, Advanced/Pro plan). The inferred gross margin is **₹20-50/shipment** before packaging, the 18% GST effect and pickup costs. At 30-50 walk-ins/day (≈800-1,300/month), that is about ₹16,000-65,000/month gross. The volume range is an assumption that mirrors the counter-volume assumptions in the franchise section below.
- **D2C/SME accounts:** Online sellers can open their own Shiprocket or NimbusPost account at ₹36-45 average, so a reseller can only add about ₹5-15/shipment. To charge more, the reseller needs to add value: free pickup, packaging, NDR/RTO handling, COD follow-up or consolidated billing. Value-added margin is more defensible than rate arbitrage.
- The "average shipment cost" figures (₹36-45) are blended across zones and weights. Local and intra-city rates sit at the lower starting figures (₹19-27). National 500 g shipments cost more than average.
- Aggregator wallet promos (₹600-700 bonus on a first ₹1,000 recharge) are one-off and should not be counted as recurring margin.
- **Capital needed:** low. A laptop, label printer, weighing scale, packaging stock and wallet float of about ₹10,000-25,000 (this float figure is an assumption).
- **Main risks:** RTO and COD losses charged to the operator's wallet, weight-discrepancy penalties, aggregator ToS on reselling (unknown), price competition from direct sign-up, and GST compliance.

### Gaps
- No published reseller, white-label or franchise terms (margin, sub-account pricing) were found for Shiprocket, NimbusPost, iThink Logistics, Shipway, Delhivery One or ShipGlobal. The Delhivery One page did not render. iThink and ShipGlobal rate cards were not retrieved.
- Whether aggregator terms of service allow third-party resale (booking for walk-in customers under one account) was not verified.
- No Hyderabad-specific survey of walk-in counter prices was found. Prices should be verified by visiting 3-5 counters (e.g., in Ameerpet, Kukatpally or Dilsukhnagar).
- Per-zone rate cards per weight slab need to be pulled from logged-in aggregator dashboards.

## Q2. Courier booking counter or multi-brand courier franchise (DTDC, Blue Dart, Professional, Trackon, Shree Maruti, India Post)

### Takeaway
No brand publishes its booking commission. Franchise blogs quote 10-35% of freight for DTDC, and a 2026 guide models 25-30%. At a typical ₹120 booking and 50 parcels/day, that yields only ₹10,000-18,000/month net after rent and staff. India Post's franchise pays small fixed amounts (about ₹3-5 per article) per secondary sites. Counter income is modest and works best stacked with other streams.

### Cited Findings
- DTDC booking commission per franchise blogs, which conflict with each other: 10-20% of shipment billing value (domestic standard 10-15%, express 12-18%), 20-30% per parcel plus a per-delivery fee, and 20-35% depending on service and location. One article describes a 10% royalty on turnover instead, which is a different structure. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [NextWhatBusiness DTDC](https://nextwhatbusiness.com/dtdc-franchise/); [Abhitanri blog 2025](https://abhitanri.blogspot.com/2025/05/dtdc-franchise.html); [OKCredit DTDC](https://okcredit.in/blog/dtdc-franchise-cost-in-india/)
- 2026 courier franchise guide **[Marketing/Blog; explicitly assumption-based]** — [IndiaPost.org (unofficial site) Courier Franchise 2026](https://indiapost.org/courier-franchise):
  - Franchises typically earn 20-40% of freight on parcels they originate.
  - DTDC: investment ₹50,000-6 lakh, 20-30% margin, ₹25,000-70,000/month profit, break-even in 18-24 months. Delhivery: ₹1-5 lakh, 15-25%, ₹30,000-50,000/month, 6-12 months. Valmo: ₹50,000-5 lakh, 15-25%. India Post: ₹5,000 security deposit.
  - Entry-level booking franchise: ₹50,000-1.5 lakh all-in. Delivery franchise with a vehicle: ₹2-6 lakh. Tier-1 counter rent: ₹20,000-40,000/month.
  - Worked example (assumptions): 1,300 parcels/month × ₹120 = ₹1,56,000 gross. Commission at 25-30% is ₹39,000-47,000. Costs (rent ₹12,000, staff ₹12,000, utilities and packaging ₹5,000) are ₹29,000. **Net is ₹10,000-18,000/month.**
  - Warning sign: "registration fees" of ₹1-2 lakh demanded by phone indicate a scam.
- XpressBees franchise margins are about ₹10-20 per packet (example: ₹15/delivery, ₹20/pickup), and centres earn about ₹40,000-90,000/month gross. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [Linkdot XpressBees](https://linkdot.io/xpressbees-franchise-cost/)
- Delhivery franchise: a small pickup counter in a low-volume area earns about ₹25,000-45,000/month, and a high-volume metro hub ₹80,000-1.2 lakh. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [FranchiseBazar Delhivery 2026](https://www.franchisebazar.com/blog/invest-in-a-delhivery-franchise-2026-indias-logistics-giant). The XpressBees franchise fee is reportedly ₹10,000, and Delhivery offers delivery-centre and transport-booking-centre formats — [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/courier-franchise-businesses/)
- **India Post Franchise Outlet** per secondary sites, which disagree with each other:
  - Fixed fees of ₹5 per Speed Post article, ₹3 per registered article, ₹3.50 per money order of ₹100-200 and ₹5 above ₹200, and 5% on stamps and stationery.
  - A 20% bonus on bulk volume (thresholds conflict: 100 vs 1,000 articles).
  - An alternative description gives 7-25% of monthly business, with Speed Post Parcel tiers of 10% up to ₹5 lakh/month and 20-25% above.
  - **[Marketing/Blog]** — [Khatabook](https://khatabook.com/blog/india-post-franchise); [GovtSchemes](https://www.govtschemes.in/india-post-franchise-scheme); [FranchiseIndia 2025](https://www.franchiseindia.com/index.php/insights/en/article/india-post-government-franchise-cost-profit-how-to-start-in-2025.56921)
- India Post launched a new franchisee scheme from 1 Feb 2024 that allows kiranas and small shops to become franchisees. **[News]** — [FintechBizNews](https://fintechbiznews.com/govtregulators-authorities/india-posts-franchisee-scheme-from-feb-1-kiranas-shops-paanwalas-can-be-engaged). Official scheme page — [indiapost.gov.in Franchise](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/vacancies/franchise) **[Official; commission table not extracted]**

### Inferences
- **Per-unit economics:** On a ₹100 document or parcel booking, a 20-30% commission earns the counter **₹20-30/booking**. That is comparable to the aggregator walk-in margin in Q1, but the counter carries only one brand's pricing.
- A **multi-brand counter** (DTDC + Professional + Trackon + India Post, plus aggregator booking) mainly widens the choice of price and destination for customers. Each brand signs separately and may impose exclusivity, which should be verified.
- India Post at ₹5/article is very thin. Its value is the footfall it drives and its credibility for document customers.
- **Capital:** ₹50,000-1.5 lakh for a booking counter, per the blog estimates above. Ignore anyone demanding ₹1-2 lakh "registration" by phone.
- **Risks:** low walk-in volume, brand exclusivity clauses, cash handling, the parent brand's hold on pricing, and slow commission settlement.

### Gaps
- No official commission schedule was found for DTDC, Blue Dart, Professional Couriers, Trackon or Shree Maruti. Blue Dart is generally not a franchise model (direct-owned network), but this was not verified in this research.
- No Hyderabad-specific counter price list was found. A 2025 BHEL Hyderabad courier tender exists but is a bid format without prices — [BHEL tender](https://tenders.bhel.com/sites/default/files/SOQR_Cum_Price_bid_format-2025-06-23-02%3A00%3A50.pdf)
- The current India Post franchise commission table was not confirmed from the official PDF.

## Q3. First-mile pickup services for sellers, and reverse pickup payouts

### Takeaway
No official per-pickup payout was found. The only figure is a blog example of about ₹20/pickup for an XpressBees franchise. In practice, first-mile income for a small operator comes from bundling pickup into its own aggregator resale margin (Q1) rather than from a carrier's per-pickup fee.

### Cited Findings
- An XpressBees franchise example uses ₹15 per delivery and **₹20 per pickup**, and notes that thin margins require high volume. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [Linkdot XpressBees](https://linkdot.io/xpressbees-franchise-cost/)
- XpressBees runs an open "Become a Delivery Partner" programme. Payout terms are not public. **[Official]** — [XpressBees Delivery Partner](https://www.xpressbees.com/partner/delivery-partner)
- One DTDC franchise blog says DTDC pays "a fixed fee per delivery in the zone" on top of booking commission. No amount is given. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [NextWhatBusiness DTDC](https://nextwhatbusiness.com/dtdc-franchise/)

### Inferences
- **First-mile pickup as a bundled service:** Picking up 10-30 parcels from one seller in a single trip costs one rider about ₹100-200 per stop. That cost is inferred from the earlier ₹12-17/parcel rider cost. It is covered if the operator earns ₹5-15/parcel on the aggregator resale, so cluster pickups by industrial area (e.g., Begum Bazaar, Abids, Kukatpally).
- Reverse pickups typically pay about the same or more than a forward pickup because of higher failure rates. This is an inference and was not confirmed.

### Gaps
- No published reverse pickup payout per parcel for Delhivery, Ekart, Shadowfax, XpressBees or Valmo hub partners.
- Aggregators' own reverse-pickup charges to sellers (useful as a ceiling price) were not retrieved.

## Q4. Amazon Hub Delivery (I Have Space), Amazon Hub Counter, and carrier pickup-drop points

### Takeaway
Amazon pays a fixed fee per delivered package, weekly, but does not publish the India rate. Store partners handle about 20-60 packages/day. The only per-package figure circulating (US$2.50) is a US estimate and should not be used for India. No India payout figure was found for Amazon Hub Counter or Ekart, Delhivery or DTDC pickup points.

### Cited Findings
- I Have Space, now Amazon Hub Delivery, was launched in 2014 and has 25,000+ store partners. Partners earn "a fixed amount for each delivery", paid weekly, and must deliver on foot or by two-wheeler. **[Official]** — [Amazon India: I Have Space – extra income with zero investment](https://www.aboutamazon.in/news/operations/i-have-space-discover-an-extra-source-of-income-with-zero-investments); [Amazon seller blog](https://sell.amazon.in/seller-blog/i-have-space-by-amazon)
- Reported store-partner volumes are 20-30 packages/day, with later Amazon material citing 30-40/day, and another Amazon page 40-60/day. **[Official/Marketing]** — [Amazon India success stories](https://www.aboutamazon.in/news/operations/i-have-space-store-owners-recount-their-success-stories); [Amazon India success stories (2)](https://aboutamazon.in/news/operations/i-have-space-store-owners-success-stories)
- The US Amazon Hub Delivery estimate is about US$2.50 per package, which Axios derived from a US$27,000/year figure. It is US only, and Amazon did not confirm it. **[News — US]** — [Axios 2023](https://www.axios.com/2023/06/26/amazon-hub-delivery-local-small-biz)
- Amazon Hub Counter India is available mainly in major cities, and shopkeepers apply via Amazon's site. No payout is given. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [GoNukkad](https://www.gonukkad.com/blog/amazon-hub-counter)
- The only per-parcel counter payment found is a UK one: PayPoint CollectPlus guarantees retailers 25p per parcel. It is not applicable to India. **[News — UK]** — [Better Retailing](https://betterretailing.com/amazon-partners-paypoint-counter-service)

### Inferences
- Using the earlier ₹14-25/parcel last-mile benchmark as a proxy, 30-40 packages/day gives about ₹12,600-30,000/month gross from Amazon Hub Delivery. This is an inference, not an Amazon figure. Its main benefit is that it runs in parallel with the other streams on the same premises and with the same riders.
- A pickup/drop counter (Amazon Hub Counter, Ekart or Delhivery points) is low-income, but it drives footfall into the walk-in booking counter (Q1/Q2), which has the higher margin.
- Amazon Hub Delivery agreements may restrict delivering for competing platforms from the same store. This was not verified.

### Gaps
- Official or credible anecdotal ₹ per-package rate for Amazon Hub Delivery India in Hyderabad (2025-26).
- Amazon Hub Counter India, Ekart pickup points, Delhivery Direct points and DTDC drop points: no payouts found.

## Q5. Hyperlocal: Porter, Borzo, Rapido parcels, and the fleet-owner model

### Takeaway
Porter keeps up to 30% of the fare. Two-wheeler partners anecdotally earn ₹200-300/day part-time, and Hyderabad Porter-type delivery jobs are advertised at ₹24,000-32,000/month. Porter lets one account attach multiple vehicles, so a small fleet owner can attach vehicles and drivers. However, the Telangana gig union reports falling net earnings from fuel costs, and per-vehicle owner profit was not found. Borzo's Hyderabad pricing starts at ₹45 plus ₹10/km.

### Cited Findings
- Porter uses a revenue share, charging "up to 30%" of the billed amount depending on location, with the rest going to the driver-partner. Porter turned profitable in FY25 with revenue above ₹4,000 crore. **[News]** — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=532600); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/exclusive/exclusive-porter-turns-profitable-with-over-rs-4000-cr-revenue-in-fy25-9496810)
- Porter's partner page offers daily earnings visibility and weekly payouts. **[Official]** — [Porter Partners](https://porter.in/partners)
- A Porter 2W partner case study reports average earnings of ₹200-300/day (part-time patterns) and notes Porter has an option to add multiple vehicles under one app. **[Anecdotal]** — [GrowthX Porter 2W case study](https://growthx.club/proof-of-work/supply-chain/porter/engagement---retention-project-----porter-2w-driver-partner-app|68629ced355e2918875840f9)
- A Porter blog profiles a partner who grew to three trucks, all attached to Porter. This confirms the fleet-owner model works for trucks. **[Official/Marketing]** — [Porter blog Partner Heroes](https://porter.in/blog/?p=6611)
- A Porter e-cart pilot with Saera Auto (Delhi and Bengaluru) offered a guaranteed minimum of ₹1,100/day, with up to ₹4,000/day possible. It applies to e-carts only and was not in Hyderabad. **[News]** — [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/saera-auto-to-supply-e-carts-to-logistics-company-porter-in-delhi-and-bengaluru-123277)
- A Hyderabad Porter delivery rider job listing gives ₹24,000-32,000/month. **[Anecdotal — job board]** — [Vahan](https://www.vahan.co/jobs/porter-delivery-boy-feel-khana-hyderabad/)
- National Porter driver salary is ₹2.4-5 lakh/year. **[Anecdotal — AmbitionBox]** — cited via search result for [AmbitionBox](https://www.ambitionbox.com/profile/driver-salary/greater-noida-location) (not Hyderabad-specific)
- In 2025, the Telangana Gig and Platform Workers Union (TGPWU) asked the Labour Department to act on Porter driver conditions. It cited rising fuel prices eroding earnings and demanded a fuel-cost adjustment and toll reimbursement. **[News]** — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/porter-app-drivers-being-exploited-need-action-tgpwu-to-telangana-govt-3313041); [The Hans India](https://assets.thehansindia.com/news/cities/hyderabad/gig-workers-union-calls-for-end-to-porter-drivers-woes-1031680)
- Borzo Hyderabad lists a starting fare of ₹45 and ₹10/km in zone 1. **[Official/Marketing]** — [Borzo Hyderabad](https://borzodelivery.com/in/cities/medicine-delivery-hyderabad)
- No Rapido parcel rate card was found. Rapido appears as a courier option inside Shiprocket Quick for bike parcels in Hyderabad. **[Marketing]** — [Shiprocket Quick Hyderabad](https://www.shiprocket.in/mr/quick/bike-parcel-delivery-hyderabad/)

### Inferences
- **For a small operator, hyperlocal is mainly a resale opportunity.** Booking Porter, Borzo or Rapido via Shiprocket Quick for local sellers and adding a ₹10-30 service fee is plausible (inferred). Attaching vehicles is a way to employ idle riders between hub waves.
- **Fleet-owner on Porter (2W):** At ₹200-300/day part-time or ₹24,000-32,000/month full-time gross to the vehicle, an owner who hires a driver has little left after salary and fuel. This model works better for mini-trucks (Tata Ace), where fares are higher, but truck owner P&L was not found.
- **Capital:** a 2W is about ₹70,000-1 lakh per vehicle, and a mini-truck several lakh. Both figures are assumptions and were not sourced here.
- **Risks:** fuel price volatility (union complaints), Porter's commission of up to 30%, demand seasonality, and driver retention.

### Gaps
- Hyderabad-specific Porter mini-truck (Tata Ace) gross earnings per day/month and fleet owner net margins.
- Rapido parcel per-km pricing and captain payouts in Hyderabad.
- Porter's official 2W and mini-truck rate card for Hyderabad.

## Q6. Rider and manpower supply to quick commerce and food delivery; EV fleet leasing

### Takeaway
Platforms do use vehicle and rider vendors. Swiggy's rider page explicitly connects riders to vehicle-rental vendors. However, no public source gives a per-rider fee or margin for staffing or fleet vendors serving Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart, BigBasket or Zomato. Rider earnings average about ₹28,000/month (mid-2025). EV rental (Yulu) costs riders about ₹9,000/month including maintenance, which is a usable revenue benchmark for a small EV leasing owner.

### Cited Findings
- Average monthly earnings of delivery partners rose to nearly ₹28,000 in mid-2025, from ₹21,000 in 2021. Blinkit offered at least a 20% incentive on daily earnings during Navratri 2025. **[News]** — [Business Standard, Sep 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/quick-commerce-firms-offer-festive-incentives-to-delivery-partners-125092600259_1.html)
- Swiggy's rider page says riders can rent a bike, e-bike or cycle, and that Swiggy "may also be able to help you connect with vendors" for vehicles. This confirms a vendor channel exists. **[Official]** — [Swiggy Ride](https://ride.swiggy.com/)
- Quick-commerce platforms typically offer 8-10 hour fixed shifts, often with ESI, PF and attendance-linked bonuses. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [Ascendants](https://ascendants.in/industry_events/delivery-partner-earnings-swiggy-zomato-zepto-blinkit-2025/)
- Quick-commerce enablers primarily operate on pay-per-order or transaction-linked models (a % of GTV), and managed-store models combine fixed fees with incentives. **[News]** — [Inc42 Quick Commerce Enablers](https://inc42.com/buzz/the-quick-commerce-enablers-rapidos-super-app-dream-more/)
- Illustrative quick-commerce rider cost: ₹100/hour pay, a 20-minute round trip and ₹6 fuel give about ₹39 per trip. Per-order contribution margins are about −₹5 to +₹20 (Zepto), −₹10 to +₹15 (Blinkit) and −₹15 to +₹10 (Instamart). **[Marketing/Blog — indicative]** — [Board Infinity](https://www.boardinfinity.com/blog/zepto-blinkit-quick-commerce-operations/); [Krishna Anubhav](https://krishnaanubhav.com/blog/quick-commerce-unit-economics/)
- In April 2023, Blinkit delivery partners in Delhi NCR went on strike after a payout revision lowered earnings. This illustrates the payout-revision risk. **[News — via Inc42/Wikipedia]** — [Blinkit (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blinkit)
- **EV rental benchmark:** A rider's petrol scooter fuel costs about ₹16,000/month, while renting a Yulu costs about ₹9,000/month including maintenance (Mint, May 2025). Yulu claims 30-40% savings for delivery partners. **[News]** — [Mint via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/en/stories/newspaper/Mint-Mumbai/YULUS-SMALL-EVS-SLOW-SPEED-QUICK-DELIVERY-569650)
- Yulu has partnerships with Zomato and Zepto for rider EVs, and has launched partner-led and franchise-led city expansions (Bhopal, Zirakpur). This shows a franchise route for small EV fleet owners exists. **[News]** — [Autocar Pro – Yulu Zomato](https://autocarpro.in/news/yulu-partners-with-zomato-to-scale-usage-of-electric-vehicles-114579); [Autocar Pro – Yulu Bhopal partner-led](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/yulu-launches-partner-led-ev-mobility-service-in-bhopal-targets-last-mile-delivery-segment-132427); [Autocar Pro – Yulu Zirakpur franchise](https://www.autocarpro.in/NEWS/yulu-expands-to-north-india-with-zirakpur-launch-through-franchise-partnership-126782)
- Zypp uses a subscription and per-delivery-fee model that includes vehicle, charging, insurance and maintenance. No prices are public. **[Marketing/Blog — low authority]** — [Lapaas Voice Zypp](https://voice.lapaas.com/startup/zypp-electric/business-model/)

### Inferences
- **EV leasing per vehicle:** At about ₹9,000/month rent (Yulu benchmark, i.e., ~₹2,100/week), a small owner leasing 10 EV scooters would gross about ₹90,000/month. Battery swapping or charging, insurance, maintenance, theft and idle days come out of that. The EV purchase cost and net margin were not sourced (see Gaps).
- **Rider staffing:** Without published vendor fees, the business plan should treat rider supply as a quote-driven B2B contract. A common structure in Indian staffing is a cost-plus markup of about 5-12% on payroll, but this is general industry knowledge and was not sourced in this research. Treat it as an assumption to be validated with platform vendor managers.
- **Risks:** platform payout revisions (Blinkit 2023 strike), rider attrition, statutory liability for ESI/PF if riders are on the operator's payroll, EV battery theft or damage, and Telangana's gig-worker welfare law developments (not researched here).

### Gaps
- No public per-rider or per-order fee for fleet or staffing vendors at Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart, BigBasket, Zomato or Swiggy.
- No Zypp or Yulu weekly rental price list for Hyderabad. No EV 2W purchase or lease cost for a small owner, and no B2B fleet partner programme terms.

## Q7. Fulfilment and pick-pack for small sellers (per-order fees in India)

### Takeaway
Indian pick-pack benchmarks run from ₹10/unit (Amazon FBA standard) to about ₹25/order (seller fee model) to an ₹80/order all-in 3PL bundle (Maersk, 2023, which includes shipping). A small Hyderabad operator could charge about ₹15-30/order for pick-pack plus storage. Volume depends on landing 5-20 local sellers.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon India FBA pick-and-pack is ₹10 per unit for standard items and ₹50 for oversize, heavy or bulky items, for new sellers at the STEP "Standard" level. **[Marketing/Blog — calculator site]** — [Browntape seller fees calculator](https://browntape.com/ecommerce-seller-fees-calculator/)
- A 2026 Indian seller cost framework assumes a ₹25 pick-and-pack fee plus 18% GST on fees. **[Marketing/Blog]** — [Base.com 2026 framework](https://www.base.com/en-IN/blog/how-to-calculate-your-true-cost-per-delivered-order-in-india-2026-framework)
- Maersk announced an all-in-one e-commerce fulfilment offer for Indian SMEs at a flat ₹80 per order ("One Country, One Price", 2023). **[News, 2023]** — [Maritime Gateway](https://www.maritimegateway.com/maersk-revolutionises-ecommerce-in-india-with-one-country-one-price-fulfilment-solution/)
- Pricing models include per-order (better for multi-SKU orders) and per-pick (cheaper for single-SKU orders). **[Marketing/Blog — US generic]** — [Racklify](https://racklify.com/encyclopedia/pick-fee-vs-pick-and-pack-fee-how-they-differ-and-how-to-budget/)

### Inferences
- **Pricing:** A local operator can price pick-pack at about ₹15-25/order plus packaging at cost plus a markup, and storage per shelf or CBM. Combined with shipping resale (Q1) at ₹5-15, total revenue is about ₹25-45/order, versus the ₹80 all-in Maersk benchmark that includes freight.
- **Cost:** One packer handles roughly 150-300 simple orders/day (assumption, not sourced). At a ₹12,000-15,000/month wage (in line with the courier franchise staff cost assumption in Q2), labour is about ₹2-4/order at moderate volume. That leaves a healthy gross margin on ₹15-25 pick-pack if volume exists.
- **Capital:** racking, packing table, scale, label printer, about 300-1,000 sq ft and basic WMS software (Shiprocket, Unicommerce or similar). Amounts were not sourced.
- **Risks:** inventory shrinkage and liability, seller churn, the need for GST registration for sellers' additional place of business (for marketplace sellers), and seasonality.

### Gaps
- No published per-order pick-pack rate cards from Indian 3PLs (Shiprocket Fulfilment, Delhivery, WareIQ, Prozo) were retrieved, and no Hyderabad warehousing rent per sq ft.
- Current status of the Maersk ₹80 offer was not verified for 2025-26.
