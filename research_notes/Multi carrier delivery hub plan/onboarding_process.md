# Onboarding Process for Last-Mile / Delivery-Hub / Franchise Partners with Indian Logistics Companies (2025-2026), and Multi-Carrier Exclusivity

Scope note: Hyderabad (Telangana) context. Researched 6 Nov 2026. "Official" = the company's own site, app documents or press. "Third-party" = blogs, franchise portals, job sites. Most per-company process detail is NOT published officially; where only third-party sources exist, this is flagged. Rates deliberately not covered.

## 1. Application channel and step-by-step process per company (first application to first parcel and payment)

### Takeaway
Only Shadowfax, Ekart (booking franchise), Amazon (IHS / DSP) and Valmo publish an official entry point. The common flow everywhere is: online form or app sign-up, a call from a city or area team, a site visit plus PIN-code / territory availability check, KYC upload, signing the agreement (often a click-through in the partner app), app/ERP login and training, then first load. No company publishes an official end-to-end timeline. The only figures found are 12-15 days (Shadowfax, older third-party), 3-5 business days for an application decision (Amazon Hub Delivery, US), and "30 minutes" for an individual rider's app KYC (Shadowfax).

### Cited Findings

**Shadowfax (official pages)**
- Channel: "Become a Franchise Partner" Google Form on shadowfax.in/franchise-partner, or email franchise.onboarding@shadowfax.in with your name, city/state, phone, email and business background. Shadowfax "reviews your application and then guides you through the next steps" — [Shadowfax blog: How to start a Shadowfax franchise (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/blogs/how-to-start-a-shadowfax-franchise-a-step-by-step-guide); [Shadowfax Franchise Partner page (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/franchise-partner)
- There are two partner types. A Channel Partner (booking counter) needs a visible store, a PC with internet and the ability to bring in shipping customers. A Delivery Franchise Partner needs "a suitable operating facility in the assigned territory," must be able to manage a delivery team, and needs a PC/internet/centre setup. Partners get a dedicated relationship manager and use an "Advanced ERP Portal" — [Shadowfax Franchise Partner page (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/franchise-partner)
- Shadowfax states it "does not charge any fee for franchise enquiries or partnership." It sends email only from @shadowfax.in addresses — [Shadowfax Franchise Partner page (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/franchise-partner); [Shadowfax blog (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/blogs/how-to-start-a-shadowfax-franchise-a-step-by-step-guide)
- Individual riders can reportedly finish onboarding "within 30 minutes" by installing the Shadowfax app and uploading PAN and Aadhaar. This came via search-result summaries of Shadowfax pages and was not fetched directly — [Shadowfax Franchise Partner page](https://www.shadowfax.in/franchise-partner)
- After evaluation, Shadowfax appoints one partner exclusively to a mutually agreed PIN-code area. One article says the whole process takes 12-15 days (third-party, older) — [FranchiseIndia: Shadowfax brand page (third-party)](https://www.franchiseindia.com/brands/shadowfax-technologies-private-limited.57551)

**Valmo (Meesho) (official app documents and site)**
- valmo.in invites visitors to "partner as a delivery partner." Owners of first- and last-mile hubs are called "Captains" ("people who own the first/last mile hubs and are responsible for all operations happening in these hubs"). Pickup/delivery executives are "Pilots" — [Valmo About (official)](https://www.valmo.in/about); [valmo.in (official)](https://valmo.in)
- Registration runs through the Valmo partner app. The app's Terms cover Delivery Pilot and Franchise Hub Captain registrations, and Users must "provide accurate, current and complete information during the onboarding process" — [Valmo App Terms & Conditions v3.0 (official)](https://www-int.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-terms-and-conditions-v3.0.html)
- Anti-fraud clause: "The Company's authorized representatives will never contact the Users to demand money for allocation of hubs or other business opportunities with Valmo" — [Valmo App T&C v3.0 (official)](https://www-int.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-terms-and-conditions-v3.0.html)
- Third-party guidance: apply via the website's Contact Us form and submit Aadhaar, PAN, address proof and business details. There is "no standalone franchise fee," but the partner bears setup, branding and operating costs — [NextWhatBusiness: Valmo franchise (third-party)](https://nextwhatbusiness.com/valmo-delivery-franchise)
- A Scribd document titled "Valmo Franchise Approval letter" exists. Fake approval letters are a known fraud pattern, so this is unverified — [Scribd (unverified)](https://www.scribd.com/document/918992772/Valmo-Franchise-Approval-letter-Abhishek)
- Corporate context: Meesho incorporated a wholly owned subsidiary, Valmo Transportation, on 28 Jan 2026 as a logistics service provider — [Arihant Capital company news](https://www.arihantcapital.com/company-information/companynewsdetails/25029010386)

**Ekart (Flipkart) (official page plus third-party)**

  The page states "no franchise fees, no security deposits, and no royalty charges." Partners get a base rate card, a dedicated account manager and API integration — [Ekart Logistics franchise/booking partner page (official)](https://www.ekartlogistics.in/?p=4036)

**Porter (hyperlocal, official)**
- Driver-partners install the Porter partner app, enter details and complete in-app training videos. Documents are vehicle RC, fitness certificate, insurance, PUC, DL, PAN, bank details and address proof. Porter has a Hyderabad presence (porter.in/two-wheelers/hyderabad) — [Porter Partners (official)](https://porter.in/partners); [Porter Hyderabad 2-wheelers (official)](https://porter.in/two-wheelers/hyderabad)

### Inferences
- A realistic Hyderabad sequence for a hub-type partnership (Shadowfax / Valmo / XpressBees / Delhivery LDF) runs as follows. Online form, then a call from the city or area ops team (days to weeks, depending on whether that PIN cluster needs a partner), then a site visit and PIN-code check, then KYC and blank cheques/deposit, then agreement or app click-through, then ERP/app login and rider IDs, then first load. The 12-15 day Shadowfax figure is the only public benchmark. Approval is demand-driven: companies onboard when a PIN code is under-served, not on a first-come basis.
- For riders and gig channels (Shadowfax rider app, Amazon Flex, Porter, Ekart Wishmaster), onboarding is app-based KYC that takes hours to days. A multi-carrier operator can register their own riders on these individually, but the riders then contract directly with the platform, not with the operator.

### Gaps
- No official XpressBees, Blue Dart, DTDC (India) or Delhivery partner process page could be read. The Delhivery page is JS-rendered, and no Blue Dart franchise or partner channel was found at all.
- No official timelines for any company except Amazon Hub Delivery US (3-5 business days for an application decision).
- Borzo and Rapido (parcel) partner onboarding was not researched due to the tool-call budget. Both are app-based individual rider sign-ups, but this is not verified here.
- Ecom Express integration into Delhivery: prior knowledge says Delhivery acquired Ecom Express in 2025, but this was not verified in this session. How legacy Ecom Express DSP/franchise partners are being migrated is unknown.

## 2. Documents required and infrastructure checklist

### Takeaway
The common core is PAN, Aadhaar, bank account and cancelled or blank cheque, premises proof (ownership or rent agreement), and GST "if applicable." Shadowfax also asks for an MSME (Udyam) certificate and blank cheques as security. Shadowfax publishes the most explicit infrastructure checklist (500-600 sq ft, CCTV, scales, label printer, scanner, racks, centre manager and rider). Riders separately need DL, RC, insurance and an Android smartphone.

### Cited Findings
- **Shadowfax documents (official):** MSME certificate; Aadhaar (front and back); PAN; property documents or rent agreement; GST certificate (if applicable); blank cheques "returned at full and final settlement" — [Shadowfax blog (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/blogs/how-to-start-a-shadowfax-franchise-a-step-by-step-guide)
- **Shadowfax infrastructure (official):** 500-600 sq ft office; PC/laptop with internet; printer and scanner; at least 2 CCTV cameras; 50 kg weighing machine; thermal label printer; barcode scanner; storage racks; one centre manager and at least one delivery executive — [Shadowfax blog (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/blogs/how-to-start-a-shadowfax-franchise-a-step-by-step-guide)
- **Valmo rider/pilot documents (official agreement):** "copy of the Aadhar card, copy of the PAN card, cancelled cheque and bank account details." Also a valid vehicle registration, driver's licence, "adequate vehicle insurance coverage," an Android smartphone, and own/rent/access to a motorcycle — [Valmo App Delivery Service Agreement v1.0 (official)](https://www.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-delivery-service-agreement-v1.0.html)
- **Valmo hub (third-party):** hub space ~300-500 sq ft; base/sort-centre partners 1,000+ sq ft; age 21+ — [NextWhatBusiness (third-party)](https://nextwhatbusiness.com/valmo-delivery-franchise)
- **Ekart booking partner (official):** "KYC documents, bank details, and hub location" — [Ekart (official)](https://www.ekartlogistics.in/?p=4036)
- **XpressBees (third-party):** active GST registration certificate; cancelled cheque; 300-500 sq ft (other sources say 100-400) — [Khatabook (third-party)](https://khatabook.com/blog/xpressbees-franchise)
- **Delhivery (third-party):** Aadhaar, PAN, GST if any, premises proof (lease with minimum tenure), bank details — [EarnKaro (third-party)](https://earnkaro.com/blog/delhivery-franchise-in-india); [FranchiseBazar (third-party)](https://www.franchisebazar.com/blog/invest-in-a-delhivery-franchise-2026-indias-logistics-giant)
- **DTDC (third-party):** PAN, Aadhaar, GST, bank details, rent agreement, passport photos, plus a DD for deposit/setup fee — [IndiaPost.org (third-party, not India Post)](https://indiapost.org/dtdc-courier-franchise); [Khatabook (third-party)](https://khatabook.com/blog/how-to-get-dtdc-franchise-in-india)
- **Amazon IHS (official):** "a physical retail space with valid documents to store packages," with documents collected at the doorstep and a background check — [About Amazon India (official)](https://www.aboutamazon.in/md/amazons-i-have-space-program-supports-local-stores-and-msmes); [Shiprocket (third-party)](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/amazon-i-have-space-program/)
- **Amazon Hub Delivery (US, official):** business entity documents, tax ID, certificate of insurance, 90+ days operating — [Amazon Hub partner guide (official, US)](https://partner-essentials.hub-delivery.amazon.com/guide/what-youll-need-to-you-apply)
- **India Post:** proof of date of birth, PAN, address proof, photos; ~200 sq ft premises — [Aditya Birla Capital (third-party)](https://www.adityabirlacapital.com/abc-of-money/post-office-franchise-business)
- **Porter (official):** RC, fitness, insurance, PUC, DL, PAN, bank, address proof — [Porter Partners (official)](https://porter.in/partners)

### Inferences
- Prepare one "partner pack" usable across all carriers: PAN (personal and, ideally, a proprietorship/firm), Aadhaar, Udyam/MSME certificate, GSTIN (Telangana), current-account cancelled cheque, registered rent agreement for a commercial unit (with landlord NOC for sub-use/storage), shop & establishment registration (Telangana Shops & Establishments Act), premises photos (front signage, interior, racks, CCTV), and rider documents (DL, RC, insurance, PUC, Aadhaar, PAN, police verification). Police verification is not named in any official source found, but background checks are mentioned by Amazon and Valmo.
- Shop & establishment licence and police verification certificates were not explicitly named by any official source found, so treat them as "likely asked," not confirmed.

### Gaps
- Official document lists for XpressBees, Delhivery LDF, DTDC India and Blue Dart are not public.
- Whether any carrier requires riders' police verification certificates, versus running its own background check vendor, is unconfirmed.

## 3. Agreement structure (contractor vs franchise, tenure, notice, exclusivity, territory, rate changes, deposits, penalties)

### Takeaway
Delivery partnerships are structured as independent service-provider / contractor agreements, not true franchises. The only full public text found is Valmo's App Delivery Service Agreement. It shows a fixed term (15 Mar 2025 to 15 Mar 2026), 3 working days' termination without cause by Valmo, immediate termination for breach, unilateral rate and fee changes accepted by continued service, set-off of penalties, COD and losses against payouts, a same-day COD deposit, TDS deduction, and Bangalore jurisdiction. It contains no exclusivity or non-compete clause. Shadowfax allocates PIN-code territory exclusively to the partner, which protects the partner from Shadowfax competitors inside the network but is not a restriction on the partner.

### Cited Findings
**Valmo App Delivery Service Agreement v1.0 (official, public)** — [link](https://www.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-delivery-service-agreement-v1.0.html)
- Nature: "Valmo intends to engage individuals as independent contractors, to carry out last mile delivery ... for Customers." "You shall be providing the Services hereunder in the capacity of an independent contractor and not as an employee or agent of Valmo." The partner gets no employee benefits (group medical insurance, allowances, bonuses).
- Term and termination: runs from the engagement date, or from March 15, 2025, whichever is later, "for a period until March 15, 2026." "Valmo may terminate this Agreement at any time with or without cause by providing a notice (written or oral) of 3 (three) working days." It may also "terminate this Agreement immediately without notice" on breach.
- Payment mechanics: "Upon the successful delivery ... You shall raise an invoice for the applicable Service Amount, at specified intervals. The undisputed Service Amount shall be paid to You upon raising the invoice." Payment is by bank transfer only. Valmo may "raise credit notes on Your behalf" for disputed or owed amounts. Disputed amounts mean the partner "will raise a credit note for the Disputed Amount."
- Facilitation fee and rate changes: the partner pays Valmo a "Facilitation Fee ... as may be charged by Valmo, from time to time ... Your continued provision of Services after such notification shall be deemed ... acceptance." Valmo "may amend, modify or update the terms ... from time to time ... Continued usage ... shall constitute acceptance."
- Set-off: "Owed Amounts" include "(i) penalties set out under this Agreement, as updated from time to time; (ii) cash-on delivery (COD) amounts required to be collected and deposited ... (iii) amounts pursuant to loss or damage to shipments by You and/or the Last Mile Delivery Hub." These can be withheld from payouts. Where no payment is due, the partner reimburses "within 15 (Fifteen) days of a claim."
- COD: collect cash "as a bailee and agent and deposit the same to the Last Mile Delivery Hub on the same day on which the shipment was delivered." Cash is collected only at delivery, never before.
- Liability: "You shall be liable for all losses suffered as a result of the shipments being lost or damaged."
- Tax: payouts are made "post deduction of tax at source (TDS). In case You are non-compliant in terms of section 206AB/206AA ... appropriate higher withholding tax rates will be considered." The partner is responsible for GST registration "if applicable," and losses from incorrect tax declarations are recovered by set-off.
- Law: laws of India, courts of Bangalore, exclusive jurisdiction.
- Exclusivity: a text search of the full agreement for "exclusiv", "compet" and "solicit" found no non-compete or exclusivity obligation on the partner. "Exclusively" appears only in "benefits ... exclusively provided to the employees of Valmo."

**Valmo App Terms & Conditions v3.0 (official)** — [link](https://www-int.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-terms-and-conditions-v3.0.html)
- "The Company reserves the right to suspend or terminate the access to the Platform ... including blocking any amounts due to the User and associated account without notice" (e.g., false onboarding info).
- On fraud or breach, the Company may "withhold all amounts payable to such User; and (b) impose penalties as the Company may reasonably determine and set off such penalties."
- GST: "The requirement for registration and compliances under the GST Laws and other tax laws is the sole responsibility of the User."
- Bangalore courts; Valmo may assign obligations.
- No non-compete clause found.

**Other companies**
- Shadowfax: after evaluation, one partner is appointed "exclusively to a mutually agreed pin-code area" (third-party) — [FranchiseIndia (third-party)](https://www.franchiseindia.com/brands/shadowfax-technologies-private-limited.57551). Shadowfax takes blank cheques returned at "full and final settlement" (official) — [Shadowfax blog](https://www.shadowfax.in/blogs/how-to-start-a-shadowfax-franchise-a-step-by-step-guide)
- Delhivery: refundable deposit "after the agreement ends if all commitments are met" (third-party) — [EarnYatra](https://earnyatra.com/?p=11635)
- Ekart booking partner: no deposit/fee/royalty, a prepaid wallet model, and the partner sets its own selling price on top of the base rate card (official) — [Ekart](https://www.ekartlogistics.in/?p=4036)
- Amazon Hub Delivery (US): program terms and agreements are accepted in the Hub app after the background check, and payment terms are signed last (official, US) — [Amazon Hub guide](https://partner-essentials.hub-delivery.amazon.com/guide/guide-for-hub-partner-application); [Your commitments as a hub partner](https://partner-essentials.hub-delivery.amazon.com/guide/your-commitments-as-a-hub-partner)
- India Post: Memorandum of Agreement plus security deposit (NSC). Commission rates are set by the Department of Posts and can change — [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/business-loan/post-office-franchise-business/)

### Inferences
- Expect most carriers' hub agreements to mirror Valmo's: 11-12 month auto-renewable terms, short no-cause termination by the carrier (days to 30 days), unilateral right to revise rates, PIN codes and SLAs, penalties for SLA breach, COD shortfall and lost/damaged shipments set off against payouts, plus a security deposit or blank cheques. The bargaining power is asymmetric, so the operator should keep fixed costs flexible (short lease lock-in, rider pay linked to volume).
- The Valmo document published is the rider/pilot-level agreement. It references a separate "Last Mile Delivery Hub" counterparty, so the Captain/hub agreement is a different document that is not public.

### Gaps
- No public text of the Valmo Captain/hub agreement, Shadowfax franchise agreement, XpressBees/Delhivery/Ekart delivery-hub agreements or Amazon IHS terms was found. Their notice periods, non-compete and territory clauses are unknown.
- Penalty schedules (e.g., per-shipment loss charges, SLA penalties) are referenced as "as updated from time to time" but not published.

## 4. Exclusivity: can one owner/hub serve XpressBees + Shadowfax + Valmo + Ekart simultaneously?

### Takeaway
No public agreement found prohibits it. Valmo's published contractor agreement has no exclusivity or non-compete clause, and Shadowfax's "exclusive PIN-code" model grants exclusivity to the partner rather than imposing it on them. However, there is no documented public evidence (news or LinkedIn) of a named Hyderabad multi-brand hub operator, and the hub-level agreements that could contain such clauses are not public. Treat multi-carrier operation as permitted-unless-the-signed-contract-says-otherwise, and read each agreement before signing.

### Cited Findings
- Valmo's delivery agreement contains no exclusivity, non-compete or non-solicit clause (verified by full-text search) — [Valmo App Delivery Service Agreement v1.0 (official)](https://www.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-delivery-service-agreement-v1.0.html)
- Valmo is designed as "an orchestration layer connecting thousands of third-party logistics partners across first-mile, mid-mile, and last-mile operations," which implies independent regional partners (third-party summary of filings) — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2024/02/meesho-logistics-valmo-supply-chain-ecommerce-vidit-aatrey); valmo.in says it works "with regional players" — [valmo.in (official)](https://valmo.in)
- Shadowfax uses a crowdsourced network of 250,000+ delivery partners quarterly across 15,100+ PIN codes. The source does not say whether those partners are multi-homing — [Inventiva (third-party)](https://www.inventiva.co.in/trends/top-10-last-mile-delivery-companies-2026/)
- Amazon IHS partners are existing shopkeepers who keep running their primary business (e.g., Hyderabad mobile store owner Naresh Thotla), which shows Amazon accepts partners with other commercial activity on the same premises — [About Amazon India (official)](https://www.aboutamazon.in/md/news/operations/meet-one-of-amazons-most-tenured-i-have-space-partners-from-hyderabad)
- Amazon Hub Delivery (US) requires an email not linked to any existing Amazon Flex or DSP account, so programmes are kept separate within Amazon. This does not restrict non-Amazon work — [Amazon Hub guide (official, US)](https://partner-essentials.hub-delivery.amazon.com/guide/what-youll-need-to-you-apply)
- An extended web search for multi-client last-mile operators serving Ekart/XpressBees/Shadowfax from one hub returned only carrier rankings and no documented examples — [Shipway (search result, irrelevant)](https://blog.shipway.com/fastest-ecommerce-courier-partners-india-2026/)

### Inferences
- Practical separation each carrier will expect, inferred from contract obligations:
  - **COD.** Valmo requires same-day deposit to the hub as "bailee and agent." Run a separate COD ledger, cash box and deposit trail per carrier, and never commingle.
  - **Riders and apps.** Each carrier issues its own rider app login tied to an individual's KYC. Either dedicate riders per carrier or let a rider hold multiple IDs (allowed only if each agreement permits it). Mixed loads on one bike risk wrong-scan or POD errors, which lead to loss set-offs.
  - **Space.** Partition racks and staging zones per brand. Carriers inspect premises, and visible competing branding may trigger questions. One label printer and scanner can be shared, but separate ERP logins are needed.
  - **Liability.** Losses are charged to the hub per carrier, so CCTV coverage per zone helps dispute claims.
- Biggest risks to multi-homing: (a) undisclosed exclusivity in hub agreements (read clauses on "conflict of interest," "competing business" and "dedicated premises"); (b) carriers declining to appoint a partner already running a competitor's hub in the same PIN cluster; (c) SLA conflicts at peak (sales events coincide across Flipkart, Meesho and Amazon).
- Lower-friction multi-carrier entry: combine Ekart's booking franchise (wallet, no deposit, no exclusivity stated) and Shadowfax channel partner (booking) with one or two last-mile hub contracts. Gig platforms (Porter, Amazon Flex) contract riders individually.

### Gaps
- No public news, LinkedIn or case evidence of named Indian multi-brand last-mile hub operators was found in the tool budget. This is the biggest evidence gap.
- Exclusivity clauses in Shadowfax, XpressBees, Ekart, Delhivery and Amazon IHS hub agreements are unknown. They must be obtained from the draft agreement during onboarding.

## 5. Tax and compliance on delivery income (GST SAC 9968, registration threshold, TDS 194C, invoicing, settlement)

### Takeaway
Courier and last-mile delivery services fall under SAC 9968 (e.g., 996812 courier services) at 18% GST under forward charge. They are not GTA (which is 5%/12% with RCM and needs a consignment note). Telangana's registration threshold for service providers is ₹20 lakh aggregate turnover, but voluntary registration is practical because carriers' agreements push GST compliance onto the partner and corporate clients prefer GSTIN invoices for ITC. Carriers deduct TDS under s.194C at 1% (individual/HUF) or 2% (firm/company), or 20% without PAN. Under the Valmo model, payouts follow partner invoices, with credit notes for disputes and set-offs.

### Cited Findings
- Courier/express parcel services are classified under SAC 9968 (e.g., 996812) at 18% GST — [ClearTax: postal & courier services SAC 9968](https://cleartax.in/s/postal-courier-services-gst-rates-sac-code-9968); [Busy: SAC 996812](https://busy.in/sac-code-996812)
- Courier is a forward-charge supply at 18%. GTA (truckloads against a consignment note/LR) is 5% (no ITC) or 12% (with ITC), often RCM. "The most common mistake is treating a courier like a GTA and applying the 5% RCM rate" — [TaxClue: GST on courier](https://taxclue.in/guide/gst-on-courier); [StudyCafe: GTA under GST](https://studycafe.in/goods-transport-agency-gst-regime-11858.html). Sources conflict on niche RCM exceptions.
- Telangana threshold for service providers is ₹20 lakh. One older source says ₹10 lakh (special category), which appears outdated — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1069174); [CalcGuru FY 2025-26](https://calcguru.in/?p=3364); conflicting: [TaxGuru (older)](https://taxguru.in/?p=908928)
- Voluntary registration below the threshold is allowed. Dealing with GST-registered clients is a common reason to register — [LegalServiceIndia](https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/?p=24645); [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/gst-registration-questions-clients-ask-ca-answers)
- Carriers shift GST responsibility to the partner. Valmo: "The requirement for registration and compliances under the GST Laws ... is the sole responsibility of the User" — [Valmo T&C v3.0 (official)](https://www-int.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-terms-and-conditions-v3.0.html). Valmo can recover losses from GST non-compliance by set-off — [Valmo Delivery Service Agreement (official)](https://www.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-delivery-service-agreement-v1.0.html). Shadowfax lists the GST certificate "if applicable" — [Shadowfax (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/blogs/how-to-start-a-shadowfax-franchise-a-step-by-step-guide)
- TDS s.194C: 1% individual/HUF, 2% others, with PAN. Without PAN it's 20% (s.206AA). Thresholds: no TDS if a single payment ≤ ₹30,000 and aggregate ≤ ₹1,00,000 per FY (sources vary on inclusive vs exclusive wording). Courier payments fall under 194C — [Teachoo: 194C rates](https://teachoo.com/949/367/Section-194C-TDS-on-Contractors/category/Rates-of-TDS); [Sortingtax: TDS on courier service](https://sortingtax.com/tds-on-courier-service/); [Bajaj Finserv: TDS on transportation](https://www.bajajfinservmarkets.in/income-tax/tds-on-transportation-charges)
- The transporter exemption (fewer than 10 goods carriages plus PAN declaration) is specific to goods carriage and does not generally cover courier/delivery services — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinservmarkets.in/income-tax/tds-on-transportation-charges)
- Valmo deducts TDS and applies higher rates for s.206AB/206AA non-compliance (non-filers / no PAN) — [Valmo Delivery Service Agreement (official)](https://www.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-delivery-service-agreement-v1.0.html)
- Settlement mechanics (Valmo): partner raises an invoice "at specified intervals," undisputed amounts paid on invoice by bank transfer, disputes handled by partner credit note, penalties/COD/loss set off, facilitation fee invoiced by Valmo to the partner — [Valmo Delivery Service Agreement (official)](https://www.valmo.in/static-assets/valmo-partner-app/documents/valmo-app-delivery-service-agreement-v1.0.html). Amazon IHS pays monthly ("by the 1st week of every month") — [Shiprocket (third-party)](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/amazon-i-have-space-program/). Shadowfax mentions "daily payments, commissions, incentives, and bonuses" for partners — [Shadowfax blog (official)](https://www.shadowfax.in/blogs/how-to-start-a-shadowfax-franchise-a-step-by-step-guide)

### Inferences
- A Hyderabad multi-carrier hub billing 3-4 carriers will cross ₹20 lakh/year quickly, and GST registration (regular scheme, 18% on SAC 996812/9968, with monthly GSTR-1/3B) should be planned from day one. Carriers claim ITC, so 18% is cost-neutral to them, and an unregistered partner may be paid net or disadvantaged. Input credit is available on rent, equipment, EV leasing etc.
- Monthly cycle per carrier: carrier ERP generates a payout statement, the partner reconciles (deliveries, RTOs, penalties, COD), raises a GST tax invoice (or self-billing, if the carrier uses it), receives payment net of TDS (1%/2%), and checks TDS credit in Form 26AS/AIS quarterly. COD is remitted daily and separately, outside the service invoice.
- Operate as a proprietorship (1% TDS) or a firm/LLP/company (2% TDS). TDS is not an extra cost; it is credited against income tax.

### Gaps
- One search result said 194C rates continue under s.393(1) of the new Income-tax Act from FY 2026-27 (Income-tax Act 2025 in force from 1 Apr 2026). The specific source page was not identified, so verify section numbering with a CA.
- Whether carriers use self-billing (carrier-generated invoices) versus partner-raised invoices varies. Only Valmo's text was confirmed.
- Whether the e-commerce TCS (s.52 CGST) or s.194-O applies to payouts routed via Valmo's "facilitation" model was not researched.

## 6. What gets an applicant approved

### Takeaway
Approval depends on the carrier needing coverage in your PIN codes, plus a ready facility in that territory, a team (centre manager plus riders), vehicle access, and clean KYC/background checks. Existing local businesses (shops) are favoured for Amazon IHS.

### Cited Findings
- Shadowfax Delivery Franchise: "a suitable operating facility in the assigned territory," the "ability to manage a delivery team," basic equipment and "a focus on service quality" (official) — [Shadowfax Franchise Partner](https://www.shadowfax.in/franchise-partner). Third-party adds age 21+, 5-10 delivery partners and vehicle access — [EarnKaro (third-party)](https://earnkaro.com/blog/shadowfax-franchise-in-india)
- Amazon IHS: own a store, space for packages, manpower and off-peak time for deliveries within 2-4 km (official) — [Amazon Seller Blog](https://sell.amazon.in/seller-blog/i-have-space-by-amazon); [About Amazon India](https://www.aboutamazon.in/md/amazons-i-have-space-program-supports-local-stores-and-msmes)

- Ekart booking partner: capacity to set up basic infrastructure and staff for daily parcel operations, and willingness to accept walk-in and corporate parcels (official) — [Ekart](https://www.ekartlogistics.in/?p=4036)
