# Commercial terms Indian last-mile networks pay small delivery-hub / franchise partners (2025-2026)

Research date: 6 Nov 2026. About 18 searches and fetches. Three official partner pages could not be read: valmo.in returned HTTP 403, xpressbees.com/partner/franchise-partner returned an empty page, and yourstory.com returned 403.

**Source labels used throughout:**
- **[OFFICIAL]**: company page, press release or filing.
- **[ANALYST]**: broker or analyst summary of a filing (DRHP or annual report).
- **[NEWS]**: business press.
- **[MARKETING]**: franchise-consultant, aggregator or SEO blog. Usually unsourced, often copied between sites, often contradictory.
- **[FRANCHISEE]**: first-hand operator statement. **None found.** No Reddit or Quora franchisee statements came up in this session; YouTube transcripts were not searched.

**Main caveat for the report writer:** no network (XpressBees, Shadowfax, Valmo, Ekart, Delhivery, DTDC, Blue Dart, Trackon, Shree Maruti) publishes a per-parcel payout sheet for hub or franchise partners. Amazon publishes the structure of its programme (fixed amount per delivery, monthly payout) but not the rupee rate. Every per-parcel rupee figure below is either a [MARKETING] claim or an inference from company-level unit economics.

## 1. Per-shipment payout to the hub/franchise partner (forward, reverse, RTO, COD vs prepaid; urban vs rural; slabs)

### Takeaway
No official per-parcel rate card was found for any network. Marketing sites repeat a band of about **₹10-₹25 per delivered forward parcel**, with ₹18-₹25 the most-repeated figure for Valmo and Shadowfax. These figures are unverified and partly copied between sites. Filing-based numbers cap what is possible: the *entire* logistics cost a platform pays per order (first mile + line haul + sort + last mile) is only about **₹41-₹53**. That makes a ₹12-₹25 last-mile hub share plausible but not confirmed. No source gave separate reverse-pickup, RTO/failed-attempt, or COD-vs-prepaid rates.

### Cited Findings
**Valmo (Meesho)**
- Valmo partners earn per-order fees plus incentives tied to delivery performance targets. No rupee rate is given. [OFFICIAL] — [Valmo Partner app, Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.valmo.valmo&hl=en_IN)
- One blog says Valmo **does not publish a partner payout sheet**. It cites **₹18-₹25 per successful delivery**, attributed to a third-party guide ("The India Post", indiapost.org, which is not the government postal department). The blog states this is not an official rate. [MARKETING] — [Franchify (wixsite)](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit); underlying claim at [indiapost.org Valmo page](https://indiapost.org/valmo-courier-franchise)
- That blog's own model assumes each wasted (RTO/failed) trip costs the partner ₹10-₹15. At 200 wasted trips a month, that is about ₹2,000-₹3,000 a month. This is the author's assumption, not a Valmo term. [MARKETING] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)

**Shadowfax**
- One guide says **₹18-₹25 per e-commerce parcel**. [MARKETING] — [franchisehurt.com Shadowfax](https://franchisehurt.com/shadowfax-franchise-india/)
- An older guide (about 1.5 years old, so roughly mid-2025) says about **₹10-₹50 per parcel**, depending on distance and parcel type. [MARKETING] — [franchisehurt.com Shadowfax](https://franchisehurt.com/shadowfax-franchise-india/) (as summarised in search results)

**Ekart (Flipkart)**
- One guide says Ekart pays per package delivered, **₹10-₹50 depending on service type**, with no ongoing royalty. [MARKETING] — [nextwhatbusiness.com Ekart](https://nextwhatbusiness.com/ekart-franchise-cost-in-india/) / [7knetwork.com Ekart](https://7knetwork.com/ekart-franchise-cost/)
- A comparison site says delivery-hub franchisees earn a **pickup fee of ₹3-₹8 per parcel plus a delivery fee of ₹8-₹20 per parcel**. [MARKETING] — [FRANticc Ekart](https://franticc.com/brands/ekart-logistics) / [FRANticc compare](https://franticc.com/compare/ekart-logistics-vs-shadowfax-technologies-franchise-comparison)

**XpressBees**
- The official franchise and delivery-partner pages describe per-delivery earnings plus incentives, with no rupee figure. [OFFICIAL] — [XpressBees franchise partner](https://www.xpressbees.com/partner/franchise-partner), [XpressBees delivery partner](https://www.xpressbees.com/partner/delivery-partner)
- A blog says XpressBees "does not publish one universal investment or per-parcel payout". It adds that deliveries and pickups are summed monthly and paid to the franchisee's bank account. [MARKETING] — [juststartup.in XpressBees](https://juststartup.in/blogs/xpressbees-franchise-india-apply-cost-courier-delivery-2026)

**Generic courier franchise**
- "Small parcels: ₹10 to ₹50 per package." [MARKETING] — [biznext.in courier franchise](https://biznext.in/blog/courier-franchise-in-india/)

**Upper bound from filings: what the platform pays for the *whole* shipment**
- Meesho DRHP, via a broker note: Valmo's cost per shipped order is **₹55.6, ₹51.7, ₹46.3, ₹41.0 and ₹41.2** across successive periods (roughly FY23 to Q1 FY26). For 3PLs the figures are **₹53.5, ₹49.9, ₹48.8, ₹50.2 and ₹51.0**. [ANALYST] — [Business Standard broker report PDF](https://bsmedia.business-standard.com/_media/bs/data/market-reports/equity-brokertips/2026-01/17679504020.34145800.pdf). Column labels were unclear in the snippet; verify against the DRHP.
- JM Financial: 3PL cost per order has stayed at **₹48-₹51**. Valmo fell into the **low ₹40s in FY26**, and JM projects ₹37-₹38 by FY30. [ANALYST] — summarised in [tvsweekly substack](https://tvsweekly.substack.com/p/inside-meeshos-2300-cr-turnaround) / [zappchai substack](https://zappchai.substack.com/p/long-read-meesho-a-logistics-giant)
- Shadowfax: per-order realisation stayed at **about ₹47-₹50 from FY22 to FY25**. "Partner expenses" (payouts to riders and partners) were **63.0% of express revenue in FY25**. [ANALYST, from DRHP] — [Mann Ashar substack](https://mannashar.substack.com/p/shadowfax-tech)
- Shadowfax partner (rider) expense was **51.3% of revenue in FY24**, down from 54.7% in FY23. This is a different base from the 63% figure above. [ANALYST] — [Shaurya Yadav substack](https://shauryayadav.substack.com/p/shadowfax-technologies)
- An analyst estimate puts a platform's payment to a 3PL at about ₹53 per parcel, and a quick-commerce-type order at **₹7 vs ₹22**. This is an estimate, not a filed figure. [ANALYST] — [Equity by Piyush substack](https://equitybypiyush.substack.com/p/the-parcel-wars-shadowfax-and-the)

### Inferences
- From the Shadowfax analyst figures: 63% of about ₹47-₹50 is **about ₹30 per order paid out to all partners combined** (first-mile, line-haul and last-mile riders/partners). A last-mile hub's share of that pool falling around ₹12-₹25 is consistent with the marketing band. It cannot reach ₹40-₹50 per parcel on standard e-commerce. The **₹50 upper ends of the "₹10-₹50" ranges look like courier booking or retail prices, not last-mile hub payouts**.
- Valmo's cost per order is falling (from ₹55 toward a projected ₹37-₹38). That points to **continued downward pressure on per-parcel payouts to Valmo partners**.
- For a 3-rider hub the realistic planning range is ₹12-₹20 per *delivered* parcel, with little or nothing paid for failed attempts. That figure should be confirmed in writing from each network's local area manager. This is an inference only.

### Gaps
- No source gave separate rates for reverse pickup, RTO/failed attempt, or COD vs prepaid for any network.
- No urban vs rural slab, distance slab, or weight slab was found from any official or franchisee source.
- No Hyderabad- or Telangana-specific payout data was found.
- **DTDC, Blue Dart, Trackon and Shree Maruti:** no delivery-hub payout data found. Their public franchise programmes appear to be booking-counter (commission-on-booking) models. This was not verified in this session.
- **Delhivery and Ecom Express:** no last-mile partner payout found. The Delhivery annual report's last-mile cost line was not retrieved.

## 2. Minimum guaranteed volume and typical daily volume for a new PIN-code hub

### Takeaway
No network was found to guarantee a minimum volume. Volume numbers come from marketing sites or from Amazon's official description of its store-partner programme, which is much smaller in scale. Commonly cited volumes are **50-150 parcels a day for a local Valmo hub**, about **80 a day** as an illustrative partner figure, and **25-50 parcels a day for an Amazon Hub Delivery store**.

### Cited Findings
- A Valmo local hub handles **50-150 parcels a day**. [MARKETING, attributed to askDaman] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- An example Valmo last-mile partner handles **80 parcels a day (about 2,000 a month)**. [MARKETING, attributed to indiapost.org] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- Ekart "claims potential profitability within ~1 year at 30-35 parcels/day". Unverified. [MARKETING] — [nextwhatbusiness.com Ekart](https://nextwhatbusiness.com/ekart-franchise-cost-in-india/)
- Amazon Hub Delivery (formerly "I Have Space") partners typically deliver **25-50 packages on days the store chooses to deliver**, within a **2-3 km radius**. [OFFICIAL] — [About Amazon India](https://www.aboutamazon.in/news/operations/i-have-space-discover-an-extra-source-of-income-with-zero-investments)
- Shiprocket puts the average at 20-30 packages a day. [MARKETING/secondary] — [Shiprocket blog](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/amazon-i-have-space-program/)
- Valmo network scale: **over 800,000 orders a day across 20 states (Feb 2024)**. [NEWS] — [TechCrunch, 7 Feb 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/07/meesho-taps-micro-entrepreneurs-to-plug-gaps-in-indias-supply-chain-network)
- Valmo handled **76.35 crore shipments in FY25**, out of about 158.8 crore Meesho orders. [ANALYST, from DRHP] — [zappchai substack](https://zappchai.substack.com/p/long-read-meesho-a-logistics-giant) / [tvsweekly substack](https://tvsweekly.substack.com/p/inside-meeshos-2300-cr-turnaround)

### Inferences
- Three riders at a typical 30-50 successful deliveries per rider per day need **about 90-150 parcels a day** to be fully used. That sits at the top of the reported Valmo hub range. A new PIN-code hub may not reach it early on. This is an inference; rider productivity was not sourced here.
- No minimum guarantee means the volume risk sits fully with the franchisee.

### Gaps
- No contract or franchisee statement on minimum guaranteed volume was found for any network.
- No data was found on volume ramp-up for a new hub, or on Hyderabad PIN-code volumes.

## 3. Security deposit, onboarding fees, agreement terms, infrastructure, and who hires and pays riders

### Takeaway
Deposit and fee figures exist only on aggregator and marketing sites, and they vary widely. Shadowfax ranges from ₹1.75 lakh total up to ₹10 lakh. Ekart's own page says *no* franchise fee or deposit for its counter model, while an aggregator claims a ₹1 lakh deposit plus ₹50,000 fee. No official infrastructure checklist (square footage, CCTV, computers, printer, scale) was retrieved. The sources imply the **partner hires and pays its own riders**, because payouts are per parcel to the partner.

### Cited Findings
**Ekart**
- Ekart's official franchise-operations page describes a courier-booking partner model with **no franchise fees, security deposits or royalty**. [OFFICIAL] — [Ekart franchise operations](https://www.ekartlogistics.in/franchise-operations)
- An aggregator contradicts this: **₹50,000 franchise fee, ₹2 lakh working capital, ₹1 lakh security deposit**, with per-shipment commissions at the delivery hub. [MARKETING] — [FRANticc Ekart](https://franticc.com/brands/ekart-logistics)

**Shadowfax**
- An aggregator says **about ₹10 lakh investment, ₹1 lakh franchise fee, zero royalty**. [MARKETING] — [FRANticc Shadowfax](https://franticc.com/brands/shadowfax-technologies)
- Another guide says a **refundable deposit of ₹25,000-₹50,000** (older, roughly mid-2025) and **₹1.75-₹3 lakh total**. [MARKETING] — [franchisehurt.com Shadowfax](https://franchisehurt.com/shadowfax-franchise-india/)
- The FRANticc comparison cites an "₹8 lakh capex gap" between XpressBees and Shadowfax. [MARKETING] — [FRANticc XpressBees vs Shadowfax](https://franticc.com/compare/xpressbees-vs-shadowfax-technologies-franchise-comparison)

**XpressBees**
- The deposit is "usually refunded without interest on termination". [MARKETING] — [juststartup.in](https://juststartup.in/blogs/xpressbees-franchise-india-apply-cost-courier-delivery-2026)

**Delhivery**
- Investment is quoted at **about ₹5 lakh** on one site [MARKETING] — [FRANticc Shadowfax vs Delhivery](https://franticc.com/compare/shadowfax-technologies-vs-delhivery-franchise-comparison). Another guide says ₹1.5-₹10 lakh [MARKETING] (same search results; source page not individually verified).

**Valmo**
- A blog gives illustrative total investments of **₹1 lakh (last-mile), ₹3 lakh (small hub) and ₹5 lakh (bigger hub)**. These are the author's figures with no source. [MARKETING] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- Valmo describes partners as small entrepreneurs who run sorting centres. TechCrunch gives no size or setup specifications. [NEWS] — [TechCrunch 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/07/meesho-taps-micro-entrepreneurs-to-plug-gaps-in-indias-supply-chain-network)

**Amazon Hub Delivery**
- **No upfront investment.** It needs a permanent business location with valid documents, plus time for local deliveries. No minimum space is specified. [OFFICIAL] — [About Amazon India](https://www.aboutamazon.in/news/operations/i-have-space-discover-an-extra-source-of-income-with-zero-investments)

**Who pays riders**
- The Valmo blog's profit model has a "hiring riders" case at a 15-25% net margin and an "owner-rider" case at 30-45%. Both assume the partner pays its own riders. [MARKETING] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- Amazon insures enrolled Hub Partners *and their delivery staff*: up to ₹5 lakh for accidents and ₹1 lakh for medical costs. This implies the store partner's own staff do the deliveries. [OFFICIAL] — [About Amazon India](https://www.aboutamazon.in/news/operations/i-have-space-discover-an-extra-source-of-income-with-zero-investments)

### Inferences
- On these figures, a ₹3 lakh budget fits a Valmo-style small hub, an Ekart/Shadowfax delivery hub at the low-end quotes, or Amazon Hub Delivery (no investment). It does not fit an Amazon DSP (see section 6).
- Many aggregator figures look like they mix up the **booking-counter franchise** model with the **last-mile delivery hub** model. They should not be used as the deposit for a delivery hub.

### Gaps
- No official space requirement in square feet, and no CCTV, computer, printer or weighing-scale checklist, was retrieved for any network. The Valmo and XpressBees official pages were inaccessible.
- No agreement term (length, notice period, exclusivity, non-compete) was found.
- No figures were found on rider wages or fuel costs in Hyderabad.

## 4. Deductions and penalties: lost or damaged shipments, COD shortage, SLA misses, fake attempts; who bears COD cash risk

### Takeaway
**No published penalty schedule was found for any network.** None of the sources quantify debits for lost or damaged parcels, COD shortage, SLA misses or fake-attempt penalties. The per-parcel, partner-collects model implies that COD cash custody, and the risk of shortage, sits with the partner until it is remitted. No source confirmed this.

### Cited Findings
- A targeted search found no penalty schedule for COD discrepancies, late deliveries or RTO. — [search summary covering FRANticc, franchisehurt, biznext, nimbuspost](https://franticc.com/compare/shadowfax-technologies-vs-delhivery-franchise-comparison)
- Valmo partner earnings include incentives tied to delivery performance targets, so performance affects pay in both directions. [OFFICIAL] — [Valmo Partner app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.valmo.valmo&hl=en_IN)
- Customer-side COD charges (for example ₹30 or 2%, whichever is higher, for Shadowfax and Delhivery rate cards) are **charges to shippers, not franchisee payouts**. They should not be confused with partner income. [MARKETING/rate cards] — [biznext.in](https://biznext.in/blog/courier-franchise-in-india/) / [nimbuspost](https://nimbuspost.com/blog/shadowfax-vs-delhivery-which-is-a-better-courier-for-ecommerce-product-delivery/)

### Inferences
- With the partner's riders collecting COD cash, partners should assume that **any COD shortage, lost parcel or fraudulent "delivered" scan is debited from their payout**, typically at product or invoice value. This is common industry practice but unverified here. Confirm the debit cap and dispute process in writing.

### Gaps
- No rupee penalties, debit caps or dispute timelines were found for any network.
- No first-hand franchisee complaints about deductions were found in this session.

## 5. Settlement cycle and payment delays

### Takeaway
The only official settlement term found is Amazon Hub Delivery's: a **monthly payout on the first working day of the following month**, with incentives by the first week. One blog says XpressBees pays monthly. No data on payment delays was found.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon Hub Delivery pays monthly on the first working day of the next month, with incentive payouts by the first week. [OFFICIAL] — [About Amazon India](https://www.aboutamazon.in/news/operations/i-have-space-discover-an-extra-source-of-income-with-zero-investments)
- XpressBees sums deliveries and pickups monthly and credits the franchisee's bank account. [MARKETING, about 11 months old] — [juststartup.in](https://juststartup.in/blogs/xpressbees-franchise-india-apply-cost-courier-delivery-2026)
- Valmo pays after "a settlement cycle", with no length given. [MARKETING, attributed to askDaman] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- On COD remittance *to shippers* (not partners): "Franchise-led carriers typically sit on 5 to 10 day cycles". Delhivery claims 2-day assured COD remittance to sellers. [MARKETING / OFFICIAL app listing] — [base.com blog](https://base.com/en-IN/blog/top-10-best-courier-partners-for-d2c-brands-in-india-speed-rto-rates-integration-support-2026/), [Delhivery Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/delhivery-2/reviews)

### Inferences
- A monthly settlement means the partner must pre-fund about 30-45 days of rider salaries, fuel and rent from working capital. That is a material share of a ₹3 lakh budget.

### Gaps
- No settlement cycle was found for Valmo, Shadowfax, Ekart, Delhivery, DTDC, Blue Dart, Trackon or Shree Maruti.
- No evidence on payment-delay frequency was found.

## 6. Amazon "I Have Space" (now Amazon Hub Delivery) per-parcel rates and Amazon DSP requirements

### Takeaway
"I Have Space" has been **renamed Amazon Hub Delivery** and has **over 28,000 store partners**. Amazon says partners get a "guaranteed fixed amount per delivery" but does **not publish the rupee rate**. The DSP programme is far beyond a ₹3 lakh budget: marketing estimates put it at **₹30-₹50 lakh**, with a fleet of vans plus bikes. No official liquid-asset requirement for India was found.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon Hub Delivery: over 28,000 store partners; a guaranteed fixed amount per delivery (amount not stated); 25-50 packages per delivery day within 2-3 km; no investment needed; monthly payout; insurance for partners and staff. [OFFICIAL] — [About Amazon India](https://www.aboutamazon.in/news/operations/i-have-space-discover-an-extra-source-of-income-with-zero-investments)
- Partners are paid a fixed amount per delivery, based on packages delivered, at 20-30 packages a day. [secondary] — [Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/amazon-i-have-space-program/)
- Amazon DSP India: **more than 300 entrepreneurs running 1,500 DSP stations in 750 cities and towns**. Amazon offers negotiated rates for vehicles, insurance and payroll, and "low working capital". **No rupee capital requirement is stated** (Aug 2021). [OFFICIAL/NEWS, older] — [Amazon press release 2021](https://press.aboutamazon.com/in/2021/8/amazon-launches-next-evolution-of-its-delivery-service-partner-program-in-india), [Indian Startup News 31 Aug 2021](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/amazon-launches-dsp-2-0-to-help-aspiring-entrepreneurs-start-delivery-business)
- DSP investment is estimated at **about ₹30-₹40 lakh** in one guide [MARKETING] — [GrowthJockey](https://www.growthjockey.com/blogs/amazon-franchise) — and **about ₹50 lakh** in another [MARKETING] — [Khatabook](https://khatabook.com/blog/how-to-get-amazon-delivery-franchise-in-india) / [GoNukkad](https://www.gonukkad.com/blog/how-to-get-amazon-delivery-franchise-in-india)
- One blog lists a fleet of **at least 20 vans (over 120 cu ft) and 40 bikes**. [MARKETING, unverified] — [GoNukkad](https://www.gonukkad.com/blog/how-to-get-amazon-delivery-franchise-in-india)
- Amazon India has an EV lease programme for DSPs, with over 300 DSPs using EV three-wheelers and vans. [NEWS] — [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/amazon-india-launches-ev-lease-program-for-its-last-mile-delivery-partners-117718)
- A June 2026 Telangana Today article reports Amazon expanding its DSP programme. The article was not fetched. [NEWS] — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/amazon-expands-its-delivery-service-partner-programme-to-help-aspiring-entrepreneurs)
- Amazon Flex is a separate scheme for individual riders. It was not covered by any source in this session.

### Inferences
- Amazon Hub Delivery is the only zero-investment option with an official, documented payout structure. However, it is built for an *existing shop* delivering 25-50 parcels nearby, not for a dedicated 3-rider hub.
- A DSP needs about 10-15x the stated ₹3 lakh budget.

### Gaps
- The actual Hub Delivery rupee rate per parcel was not found; it is probably disclosed only on enrolment.
- No official DSP liquid-capital figure was found, and none for 2025-2026. The Telangana Today June 2026 article was not read.
- No Amazon Flex per-hour or per-block rates for Hyderabad were found.

## 7. Valmo's advertised ₹1-2 lakh investment and ₹30k-50k a month: the per-parcel rate and volume behind it

### Takeaway
The ₹30k-₹50k figure works out as **gross** revenue of about **80 parcels a day × ₹18-₹25 × about 25 days**, not as net profit. The same sources put net at about **₹12k-₹22k a month**, and one blog's own margin maths gives even less (about ₹5k-₹12.5k). The ₹18-₹25 rate is unverified and not from Valmo.

### Cited Findings
- Gross at 80 parcels a day is **₹36,000-₹50,000 a month**, and net in a moderate-density area is **₹12,000-₹22,000 a month**. Net margin with hired riders is **15-25% of gross**. [MARKETING, attributed to indiapost.org] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- The blog notes the source's maths does not reconcile: 15-25% of ₹36k-₹50k is about ₹5,400-₹12,500, not ₹12k-₹22k. [MARKETING, self-critique] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- The author's table, with hired riders: **net about ₹3,400-₹7,800 a month at 50 parcels a day; about ₹10,100-₹23,400 a month at 150 a day**. With the owner riding: ₹6,800-₹14,100 at 50 a day and ₹20,300-₹42,200 at 150 a day. [MARKETING, author calculation] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- On a ₹3 lakh investment, a 6-9 month payback needs about ₹33k-₹50k a month *net*. [MARKETING, author calculation] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- A local hub is claimed to earn ₹50k-₹80k a month; the blog says this "doesn't fit the per-parcel math". [MARKETING, askDaman] — [Franchify](https://priteetraffictail.wixsite.com/franchify/post/valmo-delivery-partner-earnings-profit)
- Other sites claim ₹30k-₹50k a month for a hub, attributed to "Valmo's official partner information" (not verified), and ₹50k-₹1.5 lakh for a "captain". [MARKETING] — [nextwhatbusiness.com Valmo](https://nextwhatbusiness.com/valmo-delivery-franchise/), [couriershopfranchises.com](https://couriershopfranchises.com/valmo-logistics-franchise-growth-and-earnings-guide/), [franchisehurt.com Valmo](https://franchisehurt.com/valmo-courier-franchise-india/), [vineeshrohini.com](https://www.vineeshrohini.com/valmo-delivery-partner/)
- Delivery "pilots" (individual riders) are advertised at ₹15k-₹30k a month, with only a smartphone and a two-wheeler needed. [MARKETING] — [couriers-franchise.online](https://www.couriers-franchise.online/)
- Payout rates "can also change as the network matures and competition among partners increases". [MARKETING] — summarised from Valmo guides above

### Inferences
- For a 3-rider Hyderabad hub, rider pay alone (3 × about ₹15k-₹20k) would use up most or all of the ₹36k-₹50k gross at 80 parcels a day. At about ₹20 a parcel, the hub needs **roughly 150+ delivered parcels a day** before covering riders, rent and fuel leaves meaningful profit. This is an inference; rider wages are not sourced here.
- Valmo's cost per order fell from about ₹55.6 (FY23) to about ₹41 (FY26), per the Meesho DRHP via brokers. That suggests per-parcel payouts to Valmo partners have probably been squeezed since 2023. This is inferred, not confirmed.

### Gaps

## 8. Recent changes (2025-2026): rate cuts, consolidation, Ecom Express, Meesho and Valmo, Shadowfax IPO

### Takeaway
The major structural change is **Delhivery's takeover of Ecom Express**: 78.75% completed July 2025, then 99.87%, for about ₹1,369 crore. Delhivery retained only 50-55% of Ecom's volume and plans to merge networks, which reduces the number of independent last-mile buyers. **Meesho is insourcing to Valmo** at lower cost per order than 3PLs. That pulls volume away from Delhivery, Shadowfax, XpressBees and Ecom, and pushes the per-order economics down. **Shadowfax listed in January 2026** and slipped on debut over client concentration. No explicit, documented partner rate cut was found.
